## 🌌 Central Theme: "NEW FRONTIERS — From Orbit to Insight"

DevFest Indore 2026 explores the bleeding-edge intersection of intelligent software, artificial intelligence, space technology, and next-generation planetary compute:

1. **SPACE SYSTEMS**: Satellite systems & telemetry, Earth observation data processing, space technology & exploration, edge compute beyond conventional planetary infrastructure.
2. **AI AGENTS**: Autonomous AI agents, multi-agent coordination, Model Context Protocol (MCP), Google Agent Development Kit (ADK), production-grade Gemini Enterprise orchestration.
3. **PHYSICAL AI**: Robotics and intelligent hardware, Qualcomm AI Stack & on-device neural processing, Arduino-based physical demonstrations, edge AI and embodied intelligence.
4. **CLOUD AND SYSTEMS**: Google Cloud infrastructure, scalable enterprise distributed systems, open-weight language model inference with Gemma 4, real-time voice agents in production.
